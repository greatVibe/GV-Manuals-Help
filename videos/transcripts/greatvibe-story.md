### 00:53 — Why we built greatVibe

And that led us to a fairly simple question. The agents were getting incredibly capable, but
they were still largely working inside environments that had been designed for people. So what
would happen if we built the environment around the AI itself? That's really where greatVibe
came from. We built it for ourselves, quite literally we built it for ourselves. We weren't
sitting around trying to think of an AI product to launch. We were trying to figure out how to
work differently. We wanted to give frontier AI much more of the engineering work without losing
visibility, and without losing our judgment or control as the work grew and got bigger.

### 01:36 — Seeing what changed and why

And I reckon that distinction really matters, because I don't think the real problem with AI
coding is simply whether the model writes good code, because sometimes it does. Sometimes it
gets something almost right. Sometimes it gets something wrong in a way that's completely
obvious, and sometimes it gets something wrong in a way that isn't obvious at all. But that's

without starting from scratch again? Can you actually stand behind the result?

### 02:39 — Production discipline

And that's what we mean when we talk about production discipline. greatVibe isn't asking you to

responsibility is by building an engineering system around it that gives you more visibility
into what it's doing.

### 03:01 — Intent, evidence and human judgment

### 03:35 — Taking engineering into production
