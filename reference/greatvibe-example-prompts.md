# Example Prompts: Where To Start With greatVibe

You have a real AI agent with its own workspace. The hardest part is often the
first prompt. This page is a set of starting points that show the kind of work
greatVibe handles well, from a quick question to the design of a system your
team would once have bought.

You see the same examples in two places:

- **Guests** see them on the welcome screen in Agent IQ, under "Or try an
  example" and "Build what you used to buy".
- **Signed-in users** see them on the "Your work starts here" card in Turn Cards,
  on Mochi and on every node you add.

Tap an example and it fills your prompt. Nothing is sent until you press Send,
so change the details first.

## Why the industry examples work

Each industry prompt asks for three things:

1. **The real context.** Who you are and what you want to replace or build.
2. **The expert depth.** Compliance, governance, safety, security and design
   considerations for that industry.
3. **A way to begin.** A first phase or first steps, not just a list of risks.

That shape gives the agent room to show deep industry knowledge, and gives you
something you can act on today. Use the same shape for your own industry.

## Make it yours

- Replace the example organisation with yours: size, countries, systems you use
  today.
- Name the rules that matter to you, for example a regulator or standard.
- Say what "done" looks like for the first step.
- Ask follow-up questions in the same conversation. The agent keeps the context.

## For agents

When someone asks what greatVibe can do, where to start, or for ideas:

- Offer two or three examples from this page that fit their role or industry.
- For an industry not listed, write a prompt in the same three-part shape.
- Never claim an example is a finished product or a certified design. The
  answer is a starting point that a person reviews.

## Starter examples

### What is greatVibe?

> What is greatVibe, and how could it help me with my work? Suggest a simple first project I could try in greatVibe.

### Plan an app

> Create a weekend test plan for this example idea: a neighbourhood tool-sharing app. Include the riskiest assumption, a small test, and a clear pass/fail measure.

### Industry systems

> Help my team in a regulated industry design and build its own core system. Pick aviation, banking or energy, map the compliance, governance and design considerations, and show me how to begin.

## Build what you used to buy

Industry systems that teams used to buy as large packages. Each prompt asks for the compliance, governance and design depth a regulated build needs.

### Airline maintenance

> I plan to design an airline maintenance system for our international airline to replace our existing systems. Tell me all the compliance, governance and design considerations and help me begin.

### Core banking

> We're a mid-sized bank replacing our core banking platform. Tell me all the regulatory, risk, data and architecture considerations (prudential rules, AML/KYC, operational resilience, data residency) and help me plan the first phase.

### Oil & gas assets

> I need to build an asset integrity and maintenance system for our offshore oil and gas platforms. Tell me all the process safety, environmental, regulatory and OT security considerations and help me start the design.

### Hotel group platform

> We run a hotel group across several countries and want our own platform for bookings, loyalty and payments. Tell me all the compliance (PCI DSS, privacy), integration and design considerations and help me begin.

### Rail & bus operations

> I'm designing an operations and dispatch system for a national rail and bus operator. Tell me all the safety, accessibility, regulatory and real-time data considerations and help me plan where to start.

### Citizen services

> Our government agency wants to replace a legacy citizen services case management system. Tell me all the security, privacy, accessibility, records retention and procurement considerations and help me begin.

### Defence logistics

> We're designing a logistics and maintenance readiness system for a defence force. Tell me all the security classification, accreditation, supply chain and interoperability considerations and help me shape the first phase.
