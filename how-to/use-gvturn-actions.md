# Use Gvturn Actions

Use this guide when a gvturn has finished and you need to choose what to do
next.

## Read Before Clicking

Before using a footer action, read:

- The gvturn title.
- The main result.
- Any warnings, assumptions, or open questions.
- The next-action banner.
- The primary action card.
- Any metrics or review status.

Do not click a finalising action just because it is green. Green usually means
recommended, not risk-free.

## Use Quick Reactions

Quick reactions are useful when the response you want is simple.

Choose:

- `Good` when no follow-up is needed.
- `Continue` when the current direction is right.
- `Deeper` when you want more detail.
- `Rethink` when the answer seems off.
- `Verify` when the work should be checked.
- `Audit` or `Review` when the change is broad, risky, or important.
- `Commit` or `Push` only when you are comfortable finalising the current work.

Highlighted reactions are suggestions. You still decide.

## Use Action Cards

Action cards contain fuller next-step prompts.

Use the primary card when it matches what you want. Use a secondary card when
you want a different path, such as reviewing, revising, adding detail, or
capturing follow-up work.

If none of the cards fit, write your own prompt.

## Click Or Hold

When supported:

- Click an action card to send it immediately.
- Hold an action card to copy it into the input so you can edit it first.

Use hold-to-edit when a prompt needs a file name, a narrower scope, or a safer
instruction such as "do not change files yet."

## Choose Tone

Tone controls affect the next response.

Use:

- Concise for short status or simple answers.
- Detailed for complex work or review.
- Urgent for time-sensitive issues.
- Casual for low-stakes exploration.

Tone does not replace the task. If you need a different outcome, edit the
prompt or choose a different action.

## Treat Audit Signals Carefully

If a gvturn is marked audit required, pending, or needing review:

1. Read the work summary.
2. Check what changed.
3. Ask what was verified.
4. Decide whether to approve, request changes, or ask for a deeper review.

Useful prompt:

```text
Before I choose a footer action, explain what was done, what changed, what was verified, and what risk remains.
```
