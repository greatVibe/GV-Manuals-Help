# Ask Mochi For Help

Mochi works best when your request includes the outcome, the context, and the
level of action you want.

## Open A Temporary Mochi Workspace

Select **Mochi** in Quick Access. The row requests a temporary workspace and
switches when that workspace is ready. This design applies both to guests and
signed-in members. Opening Mochi from your account keeps you signed in.

The dot is grey before assignment, green when ready or answering, amber in
the final two idle minutes, and red while the workspace is terminating.
Assignment also uses amber while it is being prepared.

Mochi ends after 15 idle minutes and has a one-hour maximum. Passive reading
and polling do not keep it alive. Its workspace is disposable. Use a
development node for files or workloads you need to keep. Ending Mochi does
not sign you out of your account.

## As A Guest

Without an account, Mochi gives you 5 counted turns per rolling 3 hours.
Questions about greatVibe, Gravient or how to use the service are free (up to
20 per 3 hours) and do not use a counted turn. The turns panel shows what you
have left.

To keep going with your own workspace, sign up right in the console: tap
**Sign up** on the turns panel, or open the **Guest** menu and choose
**Sign in or register**. Plans and the trial are in
`../reference/plans-and-pricing.md`.

## Use This Shape

```text
I want: [outcome]
Use: [file, folder, notes, or topic]
Audience: [who will read or use the result]
Do: [review, summarise, draft, compare, edit, or explain]
Do not: [limits, risks, or things to avoid]
```

## Examples

```text
I want a short project update.
Use the notes in this message.
Audience: my team.
Do: separate completed work, blockers, and next steps.
Do not invent dates or owners.
```

```text
I want to understand this repo.
Use the files in the current workspace.
Do: explain the main folders and suggest what to read first.
Do not change files.
```

```text
I want a safer spreadsheet review.
Use sample-report.xlsx.
Do: check structure, formulas, dates, and obvious sharing risks.
Do not edit the file until I approve.
```

## Say Whether Edits Are Allowed

If you only want advice, say:

> Do not change files.

If edits are allowed, say what kind:

> You may update Markdown files only.

For important documents, ask for a plan or review first.

## Ask For Evidence

Useful follow-ups include:

- "What did you read?"
- "Which file or section supports that?"
- "What are the remaining risks?"
- "What should I check manually?"

## Ask For A Better Format

You can ask Mochi to reshape the answer:

- "Make this a checklist."
- "Make this a short email."
- "Make this a table."
- "Make this suitable for a non-technical reader."
- "Give me the next three actions only."
