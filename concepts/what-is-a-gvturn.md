# What Is A Gvturn?

A gvturn is a card that records a piece of work from an assistant. It is meant
to be easier to review than a long chat message because it keeps the result,
context, and next actions together.

## What A Gvturn Usually Includes

A gvturn may include:

- A short title.
- A summary of what was done.
- Findings, recommendations, or output.
- Risks or open questions.
- Action choices for what to do next.
- A status such as completed, pending, in progress, or audit required.

## How To Review A Gvturn

Check:

- Did it answer the request?
- Did it say what it read or changed?
- Did it mention anything it could not verify?
- Are the next actions clear?
- Is human review needed before using the result?

If the gvturn changed files or made a recommendation with impact, ask for a
review summary before relying on it.

## What Choices Mean

Choices are suggested next prompts. Use them when they match what you want.

Common choices might:

- Continue the same work.
- Ask for more detail.
- Request a safer review.
- Turn the answer into a checklist.
- Approve a direction.

You can also ignore the choices and write your own follow-up.

## What The Power Footer Does

Many gvturns include a power footer below the main card. The power footer is
where you choose what happens next.

It may include:

- Quick reaction chips.
- A next-action or review banner.
- Action cards.
- Tone controls.
- Utility actions.
- Metrics and status badges.

If a gvturn feels complete but you are unsure what to do next, read the power
footer. It often shows whether the work needs review, approval, continuation,
or a follow-up question.
